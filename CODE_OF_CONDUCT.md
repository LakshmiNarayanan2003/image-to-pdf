# Code of conduct

Everyone should be able to participate regardless of background, identity or experience. Be respectful, give specific constructive feedback, assume good faith where reasonable, and make space for different perspectives.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct and sharing another person’s private information are unacceptable. Do not post sensitive images, credentials or personal documents in issues, pull requests or discussions.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close discussions, warn contributors or restrict participation to protect the project. Responses should be proportional and consistent. Report conduct concerns through a private contact method the owner lists on their GitHub profile; avoid publishing private details. No guaranteed response time is promised.

These expectations apply to project spaces and when representing the project in public. Technical disagreement is welcome when it remains focused on the work.
